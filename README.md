# HR-Analytics-Employee-Attrition-PowerBI

An interactive HR analytics dashboard developed using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary patterns, education, job roles, and employee tenure.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on extracting meaningful business insights from employee and attrition data using data visualization and business intelligence techniques.

The dashboard provides:

- Workforce overview analysis
- Employee attrition analysis
- Gender-wise attrition analysis
- Age-group attrition analysis
- Education-wise attrition analysis
- Salary-slab analysis
- Years-at-company analysis
- Job-role attrition analysis

## Key Insights

### 1. Workforce Overview

- Total employees analyzed: **1,470**
- Total attrition cases: **237**
- Overall attrition rate: **16.1%**
- Average employee age: **37 years**
- Average salary: **6.5K**
- Average years at company: **7.0 years**

### 2. Attrition by Gender

- Male employees account for **150 attrition cases**
- Female employees account for **87 attrition cases**
- The dashboard shows a higher number of attrition cases among male employees.

### 3. Attrition by Age

- The **26–35** age group has the highest attrition with **116 cases**
- The **18–25** age group has **44 cases**
- The **36–45** age group has **43 cases**
- The **46–55** age group has **26 cases**
- Employees aged **55+** have **8 cases**

The dashboard shows that attrition is concentrated mainly among employees below 45 years of age.

### 4. Attrition by Education

- **Life Sciences:** 38%
- **Medical:** 27%
- **Marketing:** 15%
- **Technical Degree:** 14%
- **Other:** approximately 5%

Life Sciences represents the largest education category among the attrition cases shown in the dashboard.

### 5. Attrition by Salary Slab

| **Salary Slab** | **Attrition Cases** |
| ---------------- | -------------------: |
| Up to 5K         | 163                  |
| 5K–10K           | 49                   |
| 10K–15K          | 20                   |
| 15K+             | 5                    |

The highest number of attrition cases is observed in the **up to 5K salary slab**.

### 6. Attrition by Years at Company

- The highest visible attrition point occurs at approximately **1 year of tenure**, with **59 cases**
- Attrition is higher during the early-tenure period
- Attrition generally becomes lower as years at the company increase

This analysis provides a basis for examining employee onboarding, experience, compensation, role expectations, and career development.

### 7. Attrition by Job Role

| **Job Role** | **Attrition Cases** |
| ------------ | ------------------: |
| Laboratory Technician | 62 |
| Sales Executive | 57 |
| Research Scientist | 47 |
| Sales Representative | 33 |

Laboratory Technician has the highest visible attrition count in the job-role analysis.
